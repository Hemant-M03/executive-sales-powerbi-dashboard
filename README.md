# 📊 Executive Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive, end-to-end Power BI dashboard designed for executive leadership to track sales performance, gross profit margins, product categories, and geographical distribution across global markets.

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this project is to provide sales executives and stakeholders with actionable insights into overall business health. The dashboard consolidates key performance metrics, monthly trends, and product-level breakdowns to enable data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** $645.22K
* **Total Gross Profit:** $296.38K
* **Gross Margin:** 45.94%
* **Total Units Sold:** 6,698
* **Total Orders:** 2,707

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Visualizations
* **Monthly Revenue & Profit Trends:** Line visual highlighting month-over-month performance fluctuations and seasonal spikes.
* **Geographical Distribution:**
  * **Top 5 Countries by Revenue:** Led by the United Kingdom, Germany, and France.
  * **Bottom 5 Countries by Revenue:** Identifies lower-performing regions (Belgium, Finland, Gambia) for targeted marketing strategies.
* **Revenue by Sales Channel:** Breakdown across Online Store, Corporate, Retail Partners, and Marketplace channels.
* **Product Category Performance:** Donut chart evaluating revenue share across Hiking, Camping, Travel, Cycling, and Fitness categories.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Slicers for Quarter, Month Name, Product Category, and Country for drill-down analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL, data cleaning, type casting)
* **Data Modeling:** DAX measures (Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Margin %, Order Counts)
* **Data Visualization:** Custom KPIs, Bar Charts, Donut Charts, Area/Line Charts, Map Visuals
